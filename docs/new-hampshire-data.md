# New Hampshire 2024–25 source audit

New Hampshire remains **source audit pending**, with no released regression dataset. Its same-year native income export is available, and an exact native-ID crosswalk now links it to authoritative school directory and offered-grade records. The public assessment file reports regular tested-denominator proficiency but still lacks the school IDs needed to join outcomes. School names are not a replacement for authoritative IDs.

## Verified income

The [official iPlatform school eligibility report](https://my.doe.nh.gov/iPlatform/Report/Report?categoryId=18&categoryName=Free+and+Reduced+School+Lunch+Eligibility&name=Free+Reduced+K-12+School+Lunch+Eligibility+Rates+by+School&path=%2FBDMQ%2FiPlatform+Reports%2FDemographic+Data%2FFree+and+Reduced+School+Lunch+Eligibility%2FFree+Reduced+K-12+School+Lunch+Eligibility+Rates+by+School) accepts school year **2025**, **Run Report**, then **Export to Excel**. A browser exported the native XLSX on October 9, 2026. The report identifies itself as **2024–2025**, final December 2, 2024, as of October 1, 2024, collected through i4See and Direct Certification.

It publishes SAU, district and school numbers, K–12 enrollment, individually eligible Free/Reduced count and an exact eligible fraction. Native school number is the needed economic identity. State/district summary rows have no school number and are excluded from any future school join. The report includes public academies, charter and interstate groups, and explains Rivendell's New Hampshire resident scope and free milk eligibility. The extract must preserve those limits. The downloaded income workbook is `data/raw/new-hampshire/income.xlsx`.

## Recovered authoritative directory crosswalk

The [official NCES 2024–25 school directory](https://nces.ed.gov/ccd/Data/zip/ccd_sch_029_2425_w_1a_073025.zip) publishes `ST_SCHID=NH-{District #}-{School #}` and a matching `ST_LEAID=NH-{District #}`. Filtering the native directory to `ST=NH` and `SCHOOL_YEAR=2024-2025` yields **518 records**. The exact numeric district/school ID pairs in the 2024–25 income workbook match **483 of 485** school records without ambiguity. Names are retained for audit but play no part in this join.

The two income records absent from the New Hampshire directory are Rivendell Academy (E), district **457**, school **29196**, and Rivendell Academy (H), district **457**, school **29197**. They remain unlinked; the income report already identifies its distinct interstate resident population. Another **35 directory records** have no matching native income school record. No income is inferred for them.

All 483 matching directory records have `NOGRADES=No`, `IGOFFERED=As reported`, and explicit Yes/No values for every grade-offered field. A conservative offered-grade screen identifies **382 grade-school configurations** without grades 9–13, ungraded or adult offerings, **77 high-school configurations**, and **24 high schools that also offer grade 13**. These are directory coverage figures, including configurations that may have no assessed grades; they do not establish tested population, exact grade enrollment, complete assessment coverage or model membership. Grade-offered flags cannot substitute for tested counts or aggregation weights.

The compact committed extract `data/source/new-hampshire-directory-crosswalk.json` retains all 518 native directory records, all 485 native income school records, the workbook's remaining title/summary/footnote rows, exact linked IDs, native source row numbers, exclusion records, stable source URLs and checksums. The income checksum remains `90161004ef11e1a486301e1b2de183b2085827f2bc485d4f5eefa45e30c68296`; the directory ZIP checksum is `39326da788aa322353d20ceaf8ad4baed26272502cd05b066cf6c594988b21ab`.

`scripts/audit_new_hampshire_directory.py` replays every link and grade classification from native fields and rejects changed source checksums, years, identifier pairs, duplicate/ambiguous identities, or mismatched derived links and coverage. It reads no assessment records and writes no canonical dataset or website export. Its four focused tests verify exact identity, name independence, same-year rejection, unavailable grade fields, high/mixed/postgraduate scope, and retained exclusions.

```sh
.venv/bin/python scripts/audit_new_hampshire_directory.py --extract
.venv/bin/python scripts/audit_new_hampshire_directory.py
.venv/bin/python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_new_hampshire_directory.py -v
```

Extraction requires the two original ignored native files; validation of the committed crosswalk runs offline. Two extractions reproduced the same bytes and checksums. The source-only crosswalk is explicitly marked `assessment_identity_pending` and `approved_for_modeling=false`.

## Assessment identity blocker

The [official 2024–25 public assessment CSV](https://www.education.nh.gov/sites/g/files/ehbemt326/files/inline-documents/sonh/publicdatadisaggregated1_0.csv), downloaded through the official assessment page, contains **90,395** rows. Its fields include `DenominatorType`, `yearid`, level of data, subject, **district name**, **school name**, grade, subgroup, FAY count ranges, four achievement percentages and `Above prof% (lvl 3&4)`. It has **no school number, district number or NCES ID column**.

The official page distinguishes the current **2024–25 Regular Denominator** file, whose denominator is the number tested, from historic 95% accountability denominator files. Current records explicitly have `DenominatorType=Regular` and `yearid=2025`. Unsuppressed schoolwide All Grades percentages could support point models only after authoritative identity and grade scope are established. The published student counts are ranges, such as `35 - 40`, and do not support exact grade aggregation weights or sampling intervals. Bounds and suppressed rates must remain unavailable. The downloaded file is `data/raw/new-hampshire/assessment.csv`.

A source-only diagnostic finds **53,386 School Level records** across **462 district/school label pairs**. For `Subgroup=All students` and `Grade=All grades`, there are 461 Math and 461 ELA records, of which 432 Math and 441 ELA rates are exact and unbounded; 430 label pairs have both exact rates. These counts precede identifier and population screening. No label pair was linked to income or the directory, and they are not eligible model counts.

The public [iAchieve dashboard](https://jwt.nh.gov/?src_route=iAchieve/AssessmentParticipation&debug_session=false&toolbar=Hidden&Width=1300px&Height=1600px) anonymously displays 2025 proficiency and has an `entityID` tooltip and download pane. However, on October 9, 2026, browser actions against the embedded dashboard consistently failed with **“Click target no longer available”**, including fresh element, visual and keyboard attempts. A directly observed Tableau view redirects to login; it was left unfilled. No token was replayed or authorization bypassed. This is an unresolved export route, not proof that the public data are absent.

Other observed official public routes are [iReport](https://jwt.nh.gov/?src_route=iReport/FrontPage&debug_session=false&toolbar=Hidden&Width=1300px&Height=1600px), [iGlossary](https://jwt.nh.gov/?src_route=iGlossary/Glossary&debug_session=false&toolbar=Hidden&Width=1300px&Height=1600px), and [iDefine](https://my.doe.nh.gov/DataDictionary/Default.aspx). The [official enrollment-by-grade report](https://my.doe.nh.gov/iplatform/Report/Report?categoryId=10&categoryName=Enrollments+by+Grade&name=Public+School+Enrollments+by+Grade&path=%2FBDMQ%2FiPlatform+Reports%2FEnrollment+Data%2FEnrollments+by+Grade%2FSchool+Enrollments+by+Grade+Public), report ID 11, returned **Internal Server Error** when retried through its public browser link on October 9, 2026. Its indexed 2025–26 default lists native school IDs and preschool/K/1–12/postgraduate enrollments; that year cannot be substituted for 2024–25. The native income-to-CCD offered-grade crosswalk above narrows the scope hold but does not repair the assessment CSV's missing identifiers.

## What resolves the hold

An official school-level assessment export containing native `School #`/`entityID` or another authoritative published assessment identifier crosswalk must link current proficiency records to the exact same-year income identities. The recovered directory establishes native income IDs and offered grades for 483 schools; it does not establish which native ID produced any particular assessment row. A future export must also verify the intended tested population and complete schoolwide grade coverage. Names, third-party name-derived school identifiers, count-range midpoints and missing-grade extrapolation cannot resolve the hold. There is no catalog descriptor or canonical import until these requirements are met. Source paths, URLs, checksums, observed headers and the recovered crosswalk coverage are recorded in `data/source/new-hampshire-audit.json` so the next audit can continue from verified files.
