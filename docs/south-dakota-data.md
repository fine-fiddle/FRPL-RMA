# South Dakota 2024–25 source audit

South Dakota remains unavailable pending a verified individual economic predictor and an actual scored-student response. Public source discovery succeeds; the obstacle is the meaning and population of the measures. No canonical observations or browser model were created.

The [2025 accountability technical manual](https://doe.sd.gov/reportcard/documents/25-Accountability-TM.pdf), dated June 23, 2025, page 16, defines economic disadvantage using an individual free/reduced-price record ending in the current year **or** attendance at a CEP/provisional school at any point during the year. Its footnote marks every such school as 100% free/reduced-price. That reporting subgroup cannot automatically become individual enrolled economic eligibility. A tested economic subgroup ratio also cannot replace an enrollment predictor.

The [2024–25 CCD final state notes](https://nces.ed.gov/ccd/xls/SY_2024-25_CCD_Final_2a_Data_Notes.xlsx) describe changed and unresolved direct-certification submission definitions. Districts shifted reported free counts in 2023–24 to direct-certification counts in 2024–25; the department is working toward clear, consistent definitions. The notes do not identify the affected school/LEA IDs, so a safe restricted set cannot be established merely by selecting numeric CCD direct-certification values. These notes are not evidence that every school is wrong, and no earlier-year predictor is substituted.

The two primary assessment documents also disagree. The [2025 calculation guide](https://doe.sd.gov/reportcard/documents/25-CalculationGuide.pdf), pages 2–3, says invalidated scores enter the current FAY proficiency denominator. The technical manual, page 41, says invalidated scores do **not** enter current or multiyear proficiency, while the 95% participation rule affects SPI points separately. Neither instruction is silently selected as the universal rule. Technical manual page 25 describes a separate All-Assessed Proficiency population without the FAY restriction and separate Not Tested/Not Scoreable categories. A native field and population mapping must resolve actual valid-score treatment before using totals or weights.

The [public school report site](https://sdschools.sd.gov/) exposes an accessible configuration and [historical 2024–25 school directory](https://sdschools.sd.gov/assets/SDAssets/202425.json) with authoritative IDs and public report links. Thus EDC's source-request note is not treated as proof that no public report exists. District statistical profiles and CEP E-Rate claiming percentages cannot replace individual school income.

The compact [source audit](../data/source/south-dakota-audit.json) records exact source URLs, local download paths, SHA-256 checksums, contradictory instructions and required resolutions. Original downloads remain ignored under `data/raw/`. Release requires an authoritative same-year individual eligibility source or identified CCD corrections/exclusions, and a native actual scored-student population whose denominator is affirmatively established. Suppression, nonparticipants and invalid scores remain distinct from zero.
