# Nebraska source audit

Nebraska is not approved for modeling. The 2024–25 native NEP downloads provide school totals with stable county/district/school identifiers, but the published performance population can include unexcused nonparticipants assigned a lowest score. A point-only model would retain that population problem.

The [NDE spring 2025 performance and participation guidance](https://www.education.ne.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/SPRING-2025-GUIDANCE-FOR-ASSESSMENT-PERFORMAN_March-2025.pdf), sections 2.3–2.6 (PDF pages 5–6), describes assigning unexcused nonparticipants the lowest performance level and calculating percentages over assigned levels. First- and second-year recently arrived English learners have different achievement treatment. Section 3.2 (page 9) describes participation counts. These are not interchangeable denominators. A coincidental equality between level counts and `TestedCount` cannot independently resolve the two population adjustments.

The public [NEP data downloads](https://nep.education.ne.gov/#/data-downloads) use `https://nepdata.education.ne.gov/api/v1/data/downloads/data-file?dataYear=20242025&fileName=<official filename>`. The official download index supplies names and dates. Native all-student school `Grade=ALL` rows are preserved in ignored raw files; the reproducible audit in [nebraska-audit.json](../data/source/nebraska-audit.json) records their checksums and count reconciliation. `NotTestedCount` contains `NA`, `*`, and numeric values. No dated primary legend establishing `NA=0` was found, so that conversion is prohibited.

An income route is available for a future restricted cohort. The [2024–25 school free/reduced workbook](https://www.education.ne.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/2024-2025_Free_and_Reduced_Lunch_Counts_by_School.xlsx?download=1) explicitly labels 223 school rows `CEP=ALL` and 990 `CEP=NONE`. Its Warning text is in an Excel drawing, not ordinary cells. It says CEP values use nutrition identified-student percentages multiplied by 1.6. All CEP rows must therefore be excluded from an individual eligibility model. Non-CEP public rows use individual ADVISER eligibility, including qualifying applications and categorical/direct certification records; published masking remains missing.

The dated [2024–25 ADVISER specification v9.1](https://education.ne.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/ADVISER_Data_Elements_20242025_v9_1.pdf), section 8.6 (PDF pages 69–70), defines individual free/reduced status and says NDE ignores individual food-service records from CEP schools. Current universal meals cannot be substituted for individual eligibility.

To unblock Nebraska, obtain a native scored-student achievement total or a primary, complete row-selection rule that removes every forced nonparticipant and resolves the EL population adjustment. Verify missing-code semantics and exact same-year school identities before importing. No adapter, intervals, history, or readiness claim is produced by this audit.
