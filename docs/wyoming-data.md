# Wyoming 2024–25 source audit

Wyoming remains on a **scientific source hold**. Its public source flow works, but the downloaded assessment file does not provide authoritative school IDs, schoolwide totals or exact valid-score weights. No Wyoming regression descriptor is published. This is evidence about the audited source routes, not a claim that suitable data do not exist.

The official [Assessment Reports page](https://edu.wyoming.gov/data/assessment-reports/) links the [WY-TOPP/WY-ALT school-level aggregate report](https://reporting.edu.wyo.gov/ibi_apps/run.bip?BIP_REQUEST_TYPE=BIP_RUN&BIP_folder=IBFS%253A%252FWFC%252FRepository%252FPublic%252FAssessment%252FAggregated%252F&BIP_item=SchoolLevelResultsHTML.htm). On October 9, 2026, the normal public browser flow selected 2024–25, all district/school/subject/grade values, XLSX and Run. It downloaded a valid 157,121-byte workbook, preserved as ignored `data/raw/wy_assessment_2025.xlsx`, SHA-256 `8b958ce24928525f0efacdfd0125bb768be168210d257301533430f3899983ba`.

The workbook has 2,023 assessment records, with individual grade values 03–10 and no all-grade school row. Its 13 columns contain school year, district/school **names**, grade, subject, number tested, participation rate, four performance-level percentages and two combined percentages. It contains no authoritative district or school code. Every native number-tested value is a protected range, such as `6 - 9`, `10 - 19` or `20 - 29`. These ranges cannot weight schoolwide aggregation. Averaging grade percentages, choosing range midpoints, or reversing rounded percentages to infer protected counts is not permitted. The native notice says results for five or fewer students are withheld and percentages are capped at 5/95; native displayed values are preserved without assuming boundary values are exact.

The [Growth and Achievement report](https://reporting.edu.wyo.gov/ibi_apps/run.bip?BIP_REQUEST_TYPE=BIP_RUN&BIP_folder=IBFS%253A%252FWFC%252FRepository%252FPublic%252FNew_Public_Reports_2024%252FAccountability%252FBubble_Report%252F&BIP_item=achievement_and_growth_bubble_report) also loads publicly for 2024–25 and both achievement-year choices. Its own displayed note restricts proficiency to students having a student growth percentile, suppresses groups or complements below ten and caps achievement at 5/95. The [official legend revised August 14, 2025](https://edu.wyoming.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Interpreting-and-Using-the-Growth-and-Achievement-Report-in-Fusion.pdf) corroborates its distinct growth-report purpose. This selected population cannot fill missing all-tested school totals.

The [public traditional performance report](https://reporting.edu.wyo.gov/ibi_apps/run.bip?BIP_REQUEST_TYPE=BIP_RUN&BIP_folder=IBFS%253A%252FWFC%252FRepository%252FPublic%252FNew_Public_Reports_2024%252FAccountability%252FPublic_School_Performance_Report%252FTraditional_School_Performance_Report%252F&BIP_item=public_traditional_school_performance_report) loads by year/district/school. The observed Snowy Range Academy 2024–25 profile displays K–8, enrollment 260 and an Achievement student count of 163, but the achievement output is a category across content areas. Separate Math/ELA goal tables display target values and Yes/No attainment, not actual subject proficiency. These counts are not independently established valid-score denominators for usable subject rates, and a tested FRPL subgroup count is not full-school income.

The [official annual technical-report folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/u/0/folders/1uJw6N3wizFsFrqa0E82OTkYJyDZDiiNC) permits ordinary public downloads. Its 2025 Math/ELA comparison reports provide statewide grade summaries; its long-term-goal report counts schools meeting targets and summarizes their student counts. Those summaries do not supply missing school-level responses or identity. The live performance report links a 2026 implementation handbook, which has not been backdated as evidence for the 2025 model definition.

The independent same-year federal source audit finds 1,604 All Student Math/ELA grade rows and **zero** G38 school totals. The historical CCD directory has 366 schools, with 293 independently reported individual direct-certification records, seven suppressed records, and 360 reported membership totals. Income availability alone does not resolve the proficiency identity/aggregation problem. Neither current directory names nor a tested-subgroup ratio can replace an exact same-year ID join.

The committed [Wyoming audit extract](../data/source/wyoming-audit.json) records observed schema, counts, examples, source URLs and full-file checksums, without publishing a model. Resolution requires native actual schoolwide Math/ELA totals with authoritative school IDs and a verified population, or complete grade rows with exact IDs and independently visible valid-score counts. Individual same-year income must then be audited separately. No CAPTCHA, secure login, name match or suppressed-count reconstruction was used.
