How to read this guide

A school year identifies the data used in a comparison. This guide does not describe newer testing contracts. Different regions and assessments can have different latest years within the same state.

Colors show test-family names documented in the included sources. A shared name does not establish equal proficiency thresholds, grade content or alternate-assessment scope. The full published definition is shown with each assessment.

Assessment owners and delivery contractors can differ. Provider information remains “Not yet audited” where the source review has not established that role. Relative target ambition also remains unaudited: no + or − marker is assigned without evidence comparing equivalent tests, grades, subjects and years.

State and district residuals describe their own model populations. Neither proficiency percentages nor studentized residuals form a common national scale.
